Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

Synonym Chain is a word game. This policy explains what information the app and website use, and how it's handled. Synonym Chain does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or other contact details.

Information stored on your device

The player name you type in and your local daily leaderboard scores are saved only in your browser's or app's local storage, on your own device. This information is never sent to us or to any server we operate, and it is not linked to your identity. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Word lookup services

To check whether a guess is a valid synonym and to show hint definitions, the game sends the word you're playing with to third-party dictionary and thesaurus services (Datamuse, Merriam-Webster, and Free Dictionary). Only the word itself is sent - no personal or device information is included in these requests.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app can show optional rewarded video ads (watch an ad for an extra life) using Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's advertising privacy policy. Ads are only shown if you choose to tap "Watch an ad" - they never play automatically.

In-app purchases (Android app)

Optional purchases (such as buying extra lives) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details; Google's own privacy policy covers how purchase and payment information is handled.

Children's privacy

Synonym Chain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact
